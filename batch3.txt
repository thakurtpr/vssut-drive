Build POST /wallets with user_id and opening_balance. It generates a wallet_id and saves the wallet to wallets.csv, allowing one wallet per user.

Build POST /transfer with from_wallet, to_wallet, and amount.

Reject the transfer if either wallet is missing (404), if the amount is not positive, if both wallets are the same, or if the balance is insufficient (409).
On success, update both balances in wallets.csv and append two rows to wallet_txns.csv that share one transfer_id.
If any write fails partway, restore the original state, so no half-completed transfer is ever saved.
Two simultaneous transfers from the same wallet must never push its balance below 0.

Done when: the total money across all wallets stays constant after any number of transfers.
